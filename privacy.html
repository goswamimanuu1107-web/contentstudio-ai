Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. ContentStudio AI is committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ContentStudio AI, we may collect the following information:

  • Name and email address (when you sign in with Google)
  • Content you generate using our AI tools
  • Usage data such as tools used and frequency of use
  • Device and browser information for technical purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our AI-powered content generation services
  • Manage your account and subscription
  • Send important updates about our service
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience

3. Google Sign-In

ContentStudio AI uses Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not have access to your Google password or other Google account data.

4. AI Content Generation

The content you input for AI generation (topics, brand names, descriptions) is sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate responses. We do not permanently store your input data beyond what is necessary to deliver the service.

5. Data Storage

Your account data is securely stored using Firebase (Google) and Supabase. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication and user management
  • Groq AI — AI content generation
  • Supabase — Database services
  • Vercel — Website hosting
  • Cashfree — Payment processing

7. Payments

Payment processing is handled by Cashfree. We do not store your credit card or payment details on our servers. All payment transactions are secured by Cashfree's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
